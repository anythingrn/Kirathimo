How units work

Each contribution buys units at the group's NAV per unit at the time it is recorded. Your units determine your pro-rata share of the group's net assets.

The public portal is read-only. Only authenticated admins can change financial records.

NAV & Unit Price

Live net asset value and the current value of one Kirathimo unit.

Formula

NAV = cash + investments + outstanding member loans − liabilities.

Current v3 tracks the first three components; a liability module can be added when Kirathimo needs it.
